Questions

The things everyone asks first.

Straight answers, no sales talk. Anything else, ask the assistant in the corner or message us on WhatsApp.

How much does a website cost in Zimbabwe?

A Rylo Labz website is $150 for Base, $250 for Premium and $300 for a full online shop. Each is paid once. After that it is $15 a month for hosting, the domain, security, backups and small changes. The full breakdown is on the pricing page.

How long does it take to build a website?

You see a working demo in 2 weeks. A Base site goes live in 2 to 3 weeks, a Premium site in 3 to 4 weeks, and an online shop in 4 to 6 weeks, depending on how fast we get your products and photos.

What is UtahOp?

UtahOp is the business management system we built. It keeps leads, quotes, invoices, the point of sale till, stock, bookings, jobs and staff in one place, on a phone or a laptop. You can try the free demo without signing up.

Do I own the code?

Yes. The code and the domain are in your name. There is no lock-in and no licence fee to keep using what was built for you.

Where is Rylo Labz based?

Bulawayo, Zimbabwe. We build for businesses in Harare, Bulawayo, Gweru, Kwekwe and across Zimbabwe, and for clients elsewhere in Africa. You can see the sites we have built and what those clients say.

How do I pay?

Half up front and half when the site goes live. Ecocash, bank transfer or card.
