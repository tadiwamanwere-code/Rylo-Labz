What it does

The work, from the first ask to the last payment.

Most businesses run on four or five separate things that do not talk: a notebook, a WhatsApp group, a spreadsheet, a receipt book. UtahOp is all of that in one place, so the same number never has to be typed twice.

01

Leads and contacts

Every enquiry lands in one list with where it came from and what was said. Call mode walks your team through the follow-up and records the outcome, so nobody gets rung twice and nobody gets forgotten.

02

Quotes, invoices and receipts

Build a quote from your own price list, send it, turn it into an invoice when it is accepted. You can see what is still owed and how old it is without opening a single spreadsheet.

03

The till

Ring up a walk-in sale on a phone, a tablet or a laptop. Search or scan a product, build the cart, take the money. Stock comes down on its own and the sale shows in the day's figures straight away.

04

Products, services and stock

One catalogue with your prices, your categories and what is left on the shelf. The till, the quotes and the invoices all read from it, so a price change happens in one place.

05

Calendar, bookings and jobs

Site visits, bookings, payments due and follow-ups on one diary the whole team shares. Jobs and projects carry their tasks, so you can see what is actually moving.

06

Money and reports

What came in, what went out, what is owed and what the month is shaping up to look like. Plain numbers, no accounting degree needed.
