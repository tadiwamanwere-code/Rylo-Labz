Questions

The things everyone asks first.

Is the website price really once off?

Yes. You pay the build price once. The only ongoing cost is the $15 a month for hosting and care.

What happens if I stop paying the $15?

The site comes offline, because nobody is paying the servers or the domain. Your code stays yours and we hand it over if you want to host it somewhere else.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The code and the domain are in your name. No lock-in.

How do I pay?

Half to start, half when the site goes live. Ecocash, bank transfer or card.

What do you need from me?

Your logo if you have one, photos of your work, and a short description of what you sell. If you do not have photos we can help.

Can I add things later?

Yes. Start on Base and move up when the business needs it. You only pay the difference.
